About Us

Our Story & Mission

Qianlan Skincare Manufacturer is a premier private label and OEM skincare producer based in Guangzhou, China. We empower cosmetic brands worldwide with high-quality, ethically manufactured skincare products.

Who We Are

Your Dedicated Skincare Manufacturing Partner

Qianlan Skincare Manufacturer was founded with a clear mission: to provide cosmetic brands with reliable, high-quality private label skincare solutions. Headquartered in Guangzhou — China's cosmetics manufacturing hub — our facility combines advanced production technology with decades of formulation expertise.

We specialize in developing and manufacturing a comprehensive range of skincare products, including cleansers, toners, lotions, creams, serums, masks, and more. Whether you're a startup launching your first product line or an established brand expanding your portfolio, our team provides end-to-end support.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 General Questions

Private label manufacturing allows you to sell skincare products under your own brand name. We produce the products according to your specifications, and you market and sell them as your own brand. This is a cost-effective way to launch a skincare line without investing in your own manufacturing facility.

Our manufacturing facility is located in Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China — the heart of China's cosmetics manufacturing industry. This strategic location gives us access to top-quality raw materials and efficient logistics networks.

Yes, but we offer flexible MOQs to accommodate both startups and established brands. MOQs vary by product type and formulation. Please contact our team for specific MOQ information for your project.

02 Quality & Certifications

Yes, all our formulations are developed to meet United States FDA standards for cosmetic products. We ensure our ingredients, manufacturing processes, and labeling comply with FDA regulations.

Yes, our Guangzhou manufacturing facility is ISO 22716 GMP-certified. This international standard ensures Good Manufacturing Practices specific to cosmetic products, covering production, control, storage, and shipment.

Absolutely. Qianlan Skincare is committed to ethical manufacturing. We do not conduct animal testing at any stage of our product development or production process. We are proud to offer cruelty-free skincare solutions.

We implement rigorous quality control at every stage — from raw material inspection to finished product testing. This includes microbiological testing, stability studies, compatibility testing, and preservative efficacy testing.

03 Product Development

Yes! Our R&D team specializes in custom formulation development. We work closely with you to understand your brand vision, target market, and desired product characteristics to create unique, effective formulas.

Development timelines vary depending on formula complexity. Generally, custom formulation takes 4-8 weeks, while using our existing formula library can be faster. Sampling, testing, and iteration time is additional.

Yes, we provide product samples for your evaluation and approval before proceeding with full-scale production. This ensures the product meets your expectations in texture, scent, efficacy, and overall quality.

04 Packaging & Logistics

Yes, we offer comprehensive packaging solutions including primary packaging (bottles, jars, tubes), secondary packaging (boxes), and label/artwork design. We can also work with your existing packaging designs.

Yes, we ship to clients worldwide. Our logistics team handles all aspects of international shipping, including documentation, customs clearance, and coordination with freight forwarders.

We offer flexible payment terms. Typically, we require a deposit to begin production with the balance due before shipment. Specific terms can be discussed during project consultation.

05 Partnership

Simply contact us through our website or email. Our team will schedule a consultation to discuss your brand vision, product requirements, target market, and budget. From there, we'll create a customized proposal for your project.

Yes, confidentiality is a top priority. All client formulas, brand information, and business details are protected by strict non-disclosure agreements. Your proprietary information remains exclusively yours.
